# Changelog

All notable changes to Ocal are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and Ocal uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-17

First public release.

### Added

- Day, week, month, agenda and tasks views, with three-day layouts for narrow windows.
- A sidebar with a mini calendar, the current event, upcoming events, calendar visibility and saved calendar sets.
- Keyboard event entry: a 15-minute time cursor, Shift ranges, and Enter to open or add.
- Shift-drag, double-click and context menus for new events.
- A floating composer with location, meeting links, guests, notes, repetition and time zone.
- An event details card beside the event, with edit, duplicate, and delete with Undo.
- Google Calendar sync for several accounts, with an offline cache and conflict checks on edits.
- Google Meet generation, and pasted Meet, Zoom, Teams or other meeting links.
- Guest invitations and Yes, Maybe or No replies.
- Local calendars and dated tasks, stored in SQLite.
- Basic iCalendar import and export.
- Omarchy theme support, a light or dark override, and zoom.
- A demo mode with an in-memory sample calendar.
